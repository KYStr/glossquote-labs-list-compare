          How it works

          Does this compare line by line?

          No. It checks whether each item exists in the other list. The two lists do not need the same order. This is not a line-by-line text diff.

          What makes two items match?

          By default, outer spaces are trimmed and matching is case-sensitive. Turn trimming off to preserve outer spaces. Ignore case uses JavaScript’s toLowerCase key. It does not normalize spelling, synonyms, NFC, or NFKC, or decide whether records represent the same person.

          What happens to duplicates in one list?

          Each list keeps the first item’s text and line number and counts duplicate lines. Shared items use A’s first spelling. The merged list keeps A’s order, then adds items from B that have not appeared in A.

          Are the lists uploaded or saved?

          The lists are calculated in this page’s memory without uploading or saving drafts. Leaving or reloading clears them. Your environment manages the OS clipboard, browser extensions, and the destination where you paste copied text.

          Does an empty result mean failure?

          No. Valid lists may have no items in a category. Even two blank lists are valid. Empty results say “No matching items” and have no content to copy.
